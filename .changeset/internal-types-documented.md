The types that public signatures name are documented, and on the reference, where they were exported and marked `@internal`: `App.Systems`, `App.Handlers`, `App.Plugins`, `App.TickError`, `App.AllHandlers` and `App.External`; `Plugin.Clashes`; and `System.TickGated`, `System.TickBody`, `System.FrameOk`, `System.FrameBody`, `System.TickEffect`, `System.LayerGated`, `System.Listing` and `System.Ordered`. `@arcadekit/three/Three` adds `Three.Entries`, the assets of a bundle a look is built `from`, and `Three.Needs`, what its drawing systems require, which `Three.instances` and `Three.objects` named and a game could not.

Nothing breaks: no export is removed or changed.
